# harness.host_design_verdicts

- Calc id, version and content hash: `harness.host_design_verdicts` v1; the hash is the entry in `src/eng_harness/calcs/calcs.lock.json`
- Status: draft (bootstrap mode, ADR-0028 section 4; owner approval awaited, spec 0007 EC-G4)
- Method source: spec 0007 section 4.4.3 with the owner's answer to Q19 (2026-11-16, option (a)): the output kind is `harness.host_design_verdicts/1` (`kinds/edit_cycle.py`: the members of `verdicts.set/1` and the calc port's `calc`, `draft` and `trace`), which the design summary reads (spec 0003 4.9). One verdict per row of `harness.csi_design_results/1`, in the row order: `source` host, `check_id` `host.<kind>_design_ratio`, `code` `host_<kind>_design`, `subject` `{frame, row frame, case row combo}` (no case when the row has no `combo`, as every concrete row). Steel: the host's `ratio`, `ratio_limit` the basis `limits.ratio_limit_ratio`, `PASS` when the ratio is at most the limit and `FAIL` above it, compared exactly, nothing recomputed (contract 4.9.1). Concrete (gap EC-G2): no single ratio in the host reply, so every row is `NEEDS_ENGINEER` with no ratio and the message "host concrete design output not mapped" in the verdict's `assumptions` (the contract verdict has no message field); the basis limit is its `ratio_limit`. The calc holds no constant and no numeric literal.
- Golden cases (`tests/data/golden/edit_cycle/`; each derived by hand in its `derivation` field, synthetic rows):
  - `verdicts-steel.json` (§4.4.3): four rows against the limit 0.95: 1.25 and 0.96 `FAIL`, 0.95 (equal to the limit) and 0.4 `PASS`, in the row order. Result: two pass, two fail.
  - `verdicts-concrete.json` (§4.4.3, EC-G2): three beam rows with no ratio column: three `NEEDS_ENGINEER`, no case, the limit 0.9 carried.
  - `verdicts-empty.json` (§4.4.3): no row gives no verdict and all counts 0.
- Boundary and contract tests: `tests/golden/edit_cycle/test_design_verdicts.py` (registration and inputs, trace, the limit boundary on both sides, the limit as stored, a row without `combo`, a zero ratio, the kind refused outside steel and concrete, a concrete row with a `ratio` column, the refusals with their fields, row order, valid payloads of the kind); `tests/contract/test_edit_cycle_kinds.py::test_fixtures` (the kind fixture and the verdict-set rules it keeps: counts, trace references, status against ratio and limit, the calc's own trace)
- Reviewer and date: awaiting review
- Findings and open gaps: (1) EC-G2 is open: every concrete frame goes to the engineer. (2) The column names `frame`, `combo` and `ratio` are those of the S-12 inventory (steel rows `{frame, ratio, ratio_type, location, combo}`) and need the local test (spec 0007 section 12); a row without a usable `frame` or, for steel, `ratio` is refused with `INVALID_ARGUMENT` naming the row, never turned into a verdict. (3) `location` and `ratio_type` are not read: spec 0007 4.4.3 gives the subject no station. (4) The kind has no `complete` member, so a reply the adapter marks incomplete gives a complete result. (5) Spec 0007 step 14 calls the calc once per design kind while `SummaryInput.verdicts` takes one reference: plan T9a's question, not decided here.
