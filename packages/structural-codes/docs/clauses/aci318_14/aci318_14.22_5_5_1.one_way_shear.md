## 1. What this check verifies (in our own words)

For an isolated footing acting as a wide beam in each plan direction, the factored shear on a section at distance `d`
from the column face must not exceed the design shear strength of the concrete alone (no shear reinforcement). The
shear is the factored soil pressure acting beyond that section; the strength is the SI one-way shear expression for
members without axial force, times the shear strength reduction factor.

## 2. Scope and applicability

| Id | Condition | If not met |
|---|---|---|
| (member scope) | Subject kind `footing` | `NOT_APPLICABLE` |
| `NORMALWEIGHT` | `concrete_class` is `normalweight` (λ = 1.0, 19.2.4.2) | `NEEDS_ENGINEER` (gap G-1: λ for lightweight concrete, Table 19.2.4.2, not implemented) |
| `FC_MIN` | `fc_MPa >= fc_min` (Table 19.2.1.1) | `NEEDS_ENGINEER` |
| `COMPRESSION` | `FZ_kN > 0` (factored column load on the footing) | `NEEDS_ENGINEER` (uplift, top reinforcement: outside this check) |
| `FULL_CONTACT` | Factored `q_min_kPa >= 0` (FS S5 with `N = FZ`) | `NEEDS_ENGINEER` (gap G-2: partial contact not implemented) |

Assumptions recorded in every verdict: no shear reinforcement (Vn = Vc + Vs with Vs = 0, eq. 22.5.1.1); column centered; rigid footing with
linear pressure (FS); net factored pressure from the column load only, the footing and soil weights balancing their own
pressure (plan §6 Q4); the subject's case is a factored (strength) case. `FC_MIN` uses the general row of Table 19.2.1.1: the structure is assigned to Seismic Design Category A, B or C, or the footing is a foundation case that the general row covers (C-2, plan §6 Q16).

## 4. Method

For direction X (sections perpendicular to X; the cantilever spans along X); direction Y swaps `Lx ↔ Ly`,
`cx ↔ cy`, `d_x ↔ d_y`, `Mby ↔ Mbx`, `Iy ↔ Ix`.

1. FS S1, S2; `q0 = FZ/A` (FS S3, strength form); preconditions `COMPRESSION`, `FULL_CONTACT` (FS S5).
2. Critical section at `d_x` from the column face (13.2.7.2 with 13.3.3.1): cantilever beyond it
   `Lc_x = Lx/2 − cx/2 − d_x`.
3. Factored shear: if `Lc_x <= 0`, `Vu_x = 0` (the section lies outside the footing); otherwise the integral of the
   linear pressure FS S4 over the region beyond the section, which for a linear field equals area times the pressure at
   the region's centroid: `x̄ = (cx/2 + d_x + Lx/2)/2`, `Vu_x = Ly·Lc_x·(q0 + |Mby|·x̄/Iy)`. The `|Mbx|` term
   integrates to zero over the full width.
4. `√f'c` used: `sf = min(√fc, sf_max)` (22.5.3.1), recorded as a step.
5. Nominal strength (eq. 22.5.5.1, SI): `Vc_x = 0.17·λ·sf·bw·d_x` with `bw = Ly`, in N, converted to kN with `k_kN`.
6. Design strength `phiVc_x = φ·Vc_x` (Table 21.2.1(b)); requirement `Vu <= φVn` with `Vn = Vc + Vs`, `Vs = 0` (eq. 22.5.1.1; the member-level strength requirement that 13.3.3.1 refers to is confirmed at C3).
7. Two verdicts, X then Y: demand `Vu_x_kN`, capacity `phiVc_x_kN` (and the Y names).

## 5. Constants

| Symbol | Value | Unit | Citation |
|---|---|---|---|
| `phi_v` | 0.75 | 1 | ACI 318-14 Table 21.2.1, row (b) shear |
| `c_vc` | 0.17 | √MPa-based coefficient | ACI 318-14 eq. 22.5.5.1 (SI) |
| `lambda_nw` | 1.0 | 1 | ACI 318-14 19.2.4.2 (normalweight concrete) |
| `sf_max` | 8.3 | MPa^0.5 | ACI 318-14 22.5.3.1 (limit on √f'c) |
| `fc_min` | 17 | MPa | ACI 318-14 Table 19.2.1.1 (minimum f'c, general) |
| `k_kN` | 0.001 | kN/N | SI prefix kilo (EC §4.7.3) |
| `k_m` | 0.001 | m/mm | SI prefix milli (EC §4.7.3) |

`sf` and `c_vc` carry the unit √MPa, which is not in `UNITS`; they are recorded with unit null and the formula names
the unit (plan §6 Q6).

## 6. Outputs

Two verdicts per evaluation (X, Y) on the footing subject: demand `Vu_x_kN` / `Vu_y_kN` (kN), capacity `phiVc_x_kN` /
`phiVc_y_kN` (kN), ratio, `ratio_limit`, status; the core marks the larger ratio governing (CF §4.5). Trace: FS steps,
`q0_kPa`, `Lc_x_mm`, `xbar_x_mm`, `Vu_x_kN`, `sf`, `Vc_x_kN`, `phiVc_x_kN`, and the Y counterparts.
