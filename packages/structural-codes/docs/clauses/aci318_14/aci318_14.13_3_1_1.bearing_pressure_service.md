## 1. What this check verifies (in our own words)

ACI 318-14 sizes the plan area of a footing with **unfactored** (service) forces and moments and the **permissible
soil pressure** chosen by geotechnical principles (13.3.1.1). The code gives no soil pressure: the permissible value is
a design-basis value set by the engineer from the geotechnical report, never a code number. The check computes the
largest service soil pressure under a rigid rectangular footing (linear pressure, full contact) and compares it with
the allowable gross pressure from the basis.

## 4. Method

### 4.1 Footing statics (shared; mechanics, not code clauses)

Reactions are forces of the support on the structure in global axes (`forces.joint_reactions/1`), so the column acts on
the footing with their negatives. Lengths are converted from mm to m with `k_m = 0.001` before the pressure equations.

- **S1** Plan area and elastic section moduli of the rectangle about its centroidal axes:
  `A = Lx·Ly`, `Sx = Lx·Ly²/6` (about X, pressure varies along Y), `Sy = Ly·Lx²/6` (about Y, varies along X),
  `Ix = Lx·Ly³/12`, `Iy = Ly·Lx³/12`.
- **S2** Moments at the centroid of the footing base, from the reaction moments plus the horizontal reactions acting at
  `e = reaction_height`: `Mbx = −MX + e·FY`, `Mby = −MY − e·FX` (moment of `r × F` with `r = (0, 0, e)` and the
  force `−F` on the footing).
- **S3** Vertical load on the soil: service check `N = FZ + W`, with `W = A·(h·γc + soil_depth·γs) + A·surcharge`;
  strength checks use the column load only, `N = FZ` (factored case), see the strength specs.
- **S4** Linear pressure at plan point (x, y) from the centroid: `q(x, y) = N/A + |Mby|·x/Iy + |Mbx|·y/Ix` on the side
  where both moment terms add (absolute values select the governing quadrant, so the sign convention of S2 affects
  only which corner governs, not the value).
- **S5** Corner pressures: `q_max = N/A + |Mbx|/Sx + |Mby|/Sy`, `q_min = N/A − |Mbx|/Sx − |Mby|/Sy`. S4 and S5 are
  valid only with full contact (`q_min >= 0`, precondition `FULL_CONTACT`).

### 4.2 Check

1. S1, S2, S3 (service form), S5 → `q_max_kPa`, `q_min_kPa` (preconditions `COMPRESSION`, `FULL_CONTACT`).
2. Demand `q_max_kPa`; capacity `q_allow_kPa` (13.3.1.1: permissible pressure from the basis).
3. Verdict through the core builder (CF §4.5): ratio `q_max / q_allow`.

## 6. Outputs

One verdict per evaluation (subject: the footing, `case`: the service case): demand `q_max_kPa` (kPa), capacity
`q_allow_kPa` (kPa), ratio, `ratio_limit` (contract default or basis `limits.ratio_limit`), status. Trace steps
(canonical units, EC §4.7.2; the m-based intermediate values are recorded through the `k_m` conversion): `A_mm2`,
`W_kN`, `N_kN`, `Mbx_kNm`, `Mby_kNm`, `Sx_mm3`, `Sy_mm3`, `q_avg_kPa`, `q_max_kPa`, `q_min_kPa`, each with its
S-equation as `source`.

## 10. Gaps and edge cases

| Id | Gap or edge case | Behavior |
|---|---|---|
| G-1 | Partial contact (`q_min < 0`): pressure redistribution over a reduced contact area | `NEEDS_ENGINEER` via precondition `FULL_CONTACT`, gap message G-1 |
| G-2 | Net allowable pressure: the overburden to subtract depends on the geotechnical definition | `NEEDS_ENGINEER` via `ALLOWABLE_GROSS` |
| E-1 | `q_min = 0` exactly | Full contact holds; normal verdict. BD-2 asserts on the recorded `q_min_kPa` step; T3 confirms its inputs give exactly 0.0 in float64 in the implemented order of operations, or adjusts the inputs (not the expectation) until they do |
| E-2 | Reaction moments with the wrong sign convention from a host | Value unaffected (S4 absolute values); S2 combination with `FX`, `FY` depends on the global-axis convention: needs local test (plan §4) |
