## 1. What this check verifies (in our own words)

In each plan direction, the factored moment of the soil pressure about the critical section at the face of the column
must not exceed the design flexural strength of the bottom reinforcement crossing that section over the full footing
width. The strength uses the rectangular concrete stress block and yielding tension steel; this version accepts only
tension-controlled sections (strength reduction factor of the tension-controlled limit) and returns `NEEDS_ENGINEER`
for sections in the transition or compression-controlled range (gap G-2).

| (body) | `eps_t >= eps_tc` in the direction evaluated (Table 21.2.2) | `NotImplementedGap` G-2 → `NEEDS_ENGINEER` |

Assumptions recorded in every verdict: column centered (concrete column, so the critical section is at its face,
Table 13.2.7.1); one layer of bottom bars per direction with `dt = d`; reinforcement spread over the full width (the
band rule for rectangular footings is detailing, not checked here, plan §6 Q7); net factored pressure from the column
load only (plan §6 Q4); the footing is not part of a special seismic system (`FY_MAX`, plan §6 Q8). `FC_MIN` uses the general row of Table 19.2.1.1: the structure is assigned to Seismic Design Category A, B or C, or the footing is a foundation case that the general row covers (C-2, plan §6 Q16).

## 4. Method

Direction X (bars spanning in X; section at the column face perpendicular to X); Y swaps `Lx ↔ Ly`, `cx ↔ cy`,
`d_x ↔ d_y`, `As_x ↔ As_y`, `Mby ↔ Mbx`, `Iy ↔ Ix`.

1. FS S1, S2; `q0 = FZ/A`; preconditions `COMPRESSION`, `FULL_CONTACT`.
2. Critical section at the column face (13.2.7.1, Table 13.2.7.1): `a_x = cx/2`, cantilever `Lp_x = Lx/2 − cx/2`.
3. Factored moment, the moment of the linear pressure FS S4 beyond the section about it, integrated exactly over the
   full width (the `|Mbx|` term integrates to zero): with `k = |Mby|/Iy`,
   `Mu_x = Ly·[q0·Lp²/2 + k·(Lp³/3 + a_x·Lp²/2)]` (lengths in m through `k_m`; kPa·m³ = kN·m).
4. Stress-block factor `β1` (Table 22.2.2.4.3): `0.85` for `17 <= fc <= 28`; `0.85 − 0.05·(fc − 28)/7` for
   `28 < fc < 55`; `0.65` for `fc >= 55`.
5. Depth of the stress block with yielding steel (22.2.2.4.1, concrete stress `0.85·fc`): `a = As·fy/(0.85·fc·b)`,
   `b = Ly`; neutral axis `c = a/β1`.
6. Net tensile strain (22.2.2.1, `eps_cu = 0.003`): `eps_t = eps_cu·(dt − c)/c`, `dt = d_x`. If
   `eps_t < eps_tc` raise `NotImplementedGap("G-2", clause="21.2.2", ...)`.
7. Nominal strength (22.3.1.1 with the assumptions of 22.2): `Mn = As·fy·(d − a/2)`, converted from N·mm to kN·m.
8. `φ = phi_tc` (Table 21.2.1 row (a) with Table 21.2.2, tension-controlled); `phiMn_x = φ·Mn`.
9. Two verdicts, X then Y: demand `Mu_x_kNm`, capacity `phiMn_x_kNm`.
