## 1. What this check verifies (in our own words)

The total longitudinal reinforcement of a nonprestressed column must lie between a lower and an upper fraction of the
gross section area. The lower limit guards against creep and shrinkage transferring load to the bars and against
too little bending resistance; the upper limit keeps the section constructible. This check compares the provided area
with both limits on the gross area.

## 2. Scope and applicability

| Id | Condition | If not met |
|---|---|---|
| (member scope) | Subject kind `frame` (column frames only, plan §6 Q1) | `NOT_APPLICABLE` |
| `STEEL_IN_SECTION` | `Ast < Ag` (CP of the [maximum axial strength spec](aci318_14.22_4_2_1.max_axial_strength.md) §2.1) | `NEEDS_ENGINEER` |

Assumptions recorded in every verdict: nonprestressed column (the clause's prestressed case is not representable);
the minimum is taken on the full gross area: the permission of 10.3.1.2 to use a reduced effective area for a column
larger than required is not used (safe side; gap G-1); the section checked is outside lap splices (plan §6 Q8).

## 3. Inputs

| Name | Unit | Physical bounds | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Ag_mm2` | mm2 | `> 0` | gross section area (B-083; `b·h` for rectangular columns) |
| `Ast_mm2` | mm2 | `> 0` | total longitudinal reinforcement (B-083) |

No load or material input: the limits do not depend on the load case (the subject's `case` is null).

## 4. Method

1. `As_min = rho_min·Ag` (10.6.1.1).
2. `As_max = rho_max·Ag` (10.6.1.1).
3. Two verdicts, minimum then maximum: (1) demand `As_min_mm2`, capacity `Ast_mm2` (`PASS` when the provided area is
   at least the minimum); (2) demand `Ast_mm2`, capacity `As_max_mm2` (`PASS` when the provided area is at most the
   maximum). The verdict builder records `ratio` and `ratio_2` (CF §4.5).

## 5. Constants

| Symbol | Value | Unit | Citation |
|---|---|---|---|
| `rho_min` | 0.01 | 1 | ACI 318-14 10.6.1.1 (nonprestressed columns, `0.01·Ag`) |
| `rho_max` | 0.08 | 1 | ACI 318-14 10.6.1.1 (`0.08·Ag`) |

## 6. Outputs

Two verdicts as in §4 step 3, with ratio, `ratio_limit`, status. Trace: `As_min_mm2`, `As_max_mm2`, `rho_ratio`
(`Ast/Ag`, for the calculation note).

## 7. Overlay

Not applicable in this pack.
