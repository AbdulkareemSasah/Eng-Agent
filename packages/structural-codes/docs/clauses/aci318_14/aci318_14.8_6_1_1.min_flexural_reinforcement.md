## 1. What this check verifies (in our own words)

In each plan direction, the bottom reinforcement provided across the footing width must be at least the minimum
flexural reinforcement for two-way slabs, a ratio of the gross section that depends on the yield strength of the bars.
13.3.3.1 makes the two-way slab provisions applicable to two-way isolated footings; C3 confirms this referral, and
the owner decides whether the beam minimum should also apply (plan §6 Q9).

## 2. Scope and applicability

| Id | Condition | If not met |
|---|---|---|
| (member scope) | Subject kind `footing` | `NOT_APPLICABLE` |
| `FY_MAX` | `fy_MPa <= fy_max` (Table 20.2.2.4(a)) | `NEEDS_ENGINEER` |

Assumptions recorded in every verdict: deformed bars (Table 8.6.1.1 rows for deformed bars only; welded wire
reinforcement is not used in footings by B-075); the gross section is the full footing width times the total depth.

## 3. Inputs

| Name | Unit | Physical bounds | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Lx_mm`, `Ly_mm`, `h_mm` | mm | `> 0` | footing geometry (B-075) |
| `As_x_mm2`, `As_y_mm2` | mm2 | `> 0` | provided bottom reinforcement spanning in X and in Y, total over the width (B-075) |
| `fy_MPa` | MPa | `> 0` | design basis `materials.rebar_fy` |

No load input: the minimum does not depend on the load case (the subject's `case` is null).

## 4. Method

Direction X (bars spanning in X, section width `Ly`); Y swaps `Lx ↔ Ly` and `As_x ↔ As_y`.

1. Gross area of the section crossed by the bars: `Ag_x = Ly·h`.
2. Minimum ratio (Table 8.6.1.1): if `fy < fy_row`, `rho_min = r1`; otherwise
   `rho_min = max(r2·fy_row/fy, r3)`.
3. `As_min_x = rho_min·Ag_x` (8.6.1.1).
4. Two verdicts, X then Y: demand `As_min_x_mm2`, capacity `As_x_mm2` (ratio `As_min / As`; `PASS` when the provided
   area is at least the minimum).

## 5. Constants

| Symbol | Value | Unit | Citation |
|---|---|---|---|
| `fy_row` | 420 | MPa | ACI 318-14 Table 8.6.1.1 (row boundary) |
| `r1` | 0.0020 | 1 | ACI 318-14 Table 8.6.1.1 (deformed bars, `fy < 420`) |
| `r2` | 0.0018 | 1 | ACI 318-14 Table 8.6.1.1 (deformed bars, `fy >= 420`) |
| `r3` | 0.0014 | 1 | ACI 318-14 Table 8.6.1.1 (lower limit, `fy >= 420`) |
| `fy_max` | 550 | MPa | ACI 318-14 Table 20.2.2.4(a) (as in the flexure spec) |

## 6. Outputs

Two verdicts (X, Y): demand `As_min_x_mm2` / `As_min_y_mm2` (mm2), capacity `As_x_mm2` / `As_y_mm2` (mm2), ratio,
`ratio_limit`, status. Trace: `Ag_x_mm2`, `rho_min_ratio`, `As_min_x_mm2` and the Y counterparts.

## 7. Overlay

Not applicable in this pack (B-072).
