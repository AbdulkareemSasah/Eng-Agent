## 1. What this check verifies (in our own words)

The factored axial compression on a short column must not exceed its design axial strength: the nominal strength of the
fully compressed section (concrete on the net area plus yielded steel), reduced by the factor for tied or spiral
columns that caps the nominal strength, and by the strength reduction factor of a compression-controlled section. It
is the pure-axial point of the interaction diagram of AF and applies whatever the moment; the axial-flexure check
covers the moment.

## 4. Method

1. Preconditions (§2.2). `Ag = b·h`.
2. Nominal axial strength (Eq. 22.4.2.2): `Po = α1·fc·(Ag − Ast) + fy·Ast`.
3. Maximum nominal axial strength (Table 22.4.2.1): `Pn_max = pnmax_tied·Po` (ties) or `pnmax_spiral·Po` (spirals).
4. Strength reduction (Table 21.2.1 row (a), Table 21.2.2): pure axial compression gives a uniform compressive strain,
   so the net tensile strain is not positive and the section is compression-controlled: `φ = phi_cc_tied` or
   `phi_cc_spiral`.
5. Design strength `phiPn_max = φ·Pn_max`, converted with `k_kN`; requirement `φPn >= Pu` (10.5.1.1(a)).
6. One verdict: demand `Pu_kN`, capacity `phiPn_max_kN`.
