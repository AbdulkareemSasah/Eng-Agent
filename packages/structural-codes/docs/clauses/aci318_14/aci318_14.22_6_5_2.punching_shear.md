## 1. What this check verifies (in our own words)

The factored shear stress on the critical perimeter around the column, at half the effective depth from the column
faces, must not exceed the design two-way shear stress of the concrete (no shear reinforcement). The shear is the
column load minus the factored soil pressure inside the perimeter; the stress capacity is the least of the three SI
expressions for two-way members without shear reinforcement, times the shear strength reduction factor. This version
handles concentric shear only: when the column transfers moment to the footing, the eccentric-shear stress is not
implemented and the check returns `NEEDS_ENGINEER` (gap G-4).

Assumptions recorded in every verdict: no shear reinforcement; rectangular column centered on the footing; `d` is the
single effective depth chosen by the caller for the two-way section (plan §6 Q5); net factored pressure from the column
load only (plan §6 Q4); the subject's case is a factored case. `FC_MIN` uses the general row of Table 19.2.1.1: the structure is assigned to Seismic Design Category A, B or C, or the footing is a foundation case that the general row covers (C-1, plan §6 Q16).

## 4. Method

1. FS S1, S2; preconditions `COMPRESSION`, `FULL_CONTACT`, `PERIMETER_INSIDE`. If `Mbx ≠ 0` or `Mby ≠ 0`, raise
   `NotImplementedGap("G-4", clause="8.4.4.2", ...)` (exact comparison with zero; no threshold is invented).
2. Critical section at `d/2` from the column faces (22.6.4.1), rectangular with sides parallel to the column:
   `b1 = cx + d`, `b2 = cy + d`, `bo = 2·(b1 + b2)`.
3. Factored shear (13.3.3.1; the two-way strength requirement of 22.6.1, whose sub-clause C3 confirms): with zero base moments the pressure is uniform, so
   `Vu = FZ·(1 − b1·b2/A)`; stress `vu = Vu/(bo·d)` (Vu converted from kN to N with `k_kN`).
4. `sf = min(√fc, sf_max)` (22.6.3.1). `β = max(cx, cy)/min(cx, cy)`. `αs = 40` (interior, 22.6.5.3).
5. Concrete stress (Table 22.6.5.2, SI): `vc = min(c_a·λ·sf, c_b·(1 + 2/β)·λ·sf, c_c·(2 + αs·d/bo)·λ·sf)`; each of the
   three is a recorded step (`vc_a_MPa`, `vc_b_MPa`, `vc_c_MPa`), and the governing one is named in a trace note.
6. Design stress `phivc = φ·vc` (Table 21.2.1(b)). One verdict: demand `vu_MPa`, capacity `phivc_MPa`.

Shared constants (B-421): `phi_v`, `lambda_nw`, `sf_max`, `fc_min`, `k_kN`, `k_m`, `c_S`, `c_I` are defined once in `aci318_14._common` (plan §1.1). Their Citation
cell is plain text equal to the constant's recorded `Cited.source`, written identically in every clause spec
that uses it (plan T2c asserts it).
Ratios of like units (`k_m`, `k_kN`, `k_kNm`) have unit 1; the citation names the ratio (plan §6 Q6).
`sf_max` limits √f'c, so its true unit is MPa^0.5; `UNITS` has no MPa^0.5, so it is stored with unit 1 and
its `Cited.note` reads "true unit MPa^0.5; stored as 1 because the eng-contract unit table has no MPa^0.5
(plan 0002 §6 Q6)". The citation names the three clauses that set the same limit; this check applies the
one of its §4 step.
`k_kN` is recorded in the trace under the name `k_kilo`: the name `k_kN` ends in the suffix `_kN`, which
fixes the unit kN (EC §4.7.2, core AC10); its `Cited` is the shared one.
`k_m`, `c_S` and `c_I` enter this check's trace through the footing statics FS S1 to S5 (bearing spec
§4.1), which the precondition `COMPRESSION` records.
